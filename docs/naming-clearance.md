# Naming & Brand Clearance Checklist

**Working name:** `Actentra` (coined working brand; not a claim of legal clearance).

Before commercial launch, paid offerings, or spending money on trademark registration or branding, complete the checklist from [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) Section 1.2:

- [ ] Search **USPTO** Trademark Search (Classes 9 & 42) for "Actentra" and phonetic variants.
- [ ] Search **Indian Trade Marks Registry** (Classes 9 & 42) for "Actentra" and phonetic variants.
- [ ] Search **EUIPO** and **WIPO Global Brand Database**.
- [ ] Check domain availability and **GitHub organization / handle** availability.
- [ ] Check **PyPI** (`actentra`) and **npm** name availability (do not squat empty placeholder packages).
- [ ] Search software directories and security tool catalogs for adjacent/conflicting names.
- [ ] Consult an IP attorney before filing trademark applications (protect name & logo, not code).
